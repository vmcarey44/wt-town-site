Visit Washington Township, NJ!

Enjoy culture and sports in this active
community close to downtown Philadelphia.

Top three activities to do in Washington Twp.

Come visit beautiful Washington Lake Park in the Heart of Washington Township. Relax and take in the scenery.

Take a day trip to the historic Old Stone House and learn about the history of the area and original founding families.

Sports fan? In the fall, visit Washington Township High School and see the Mintuemen take the field in football action.
